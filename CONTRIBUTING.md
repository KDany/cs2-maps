# Contributing

Thanks for your interest! Please note that **this repository does not accept contributions.**

- **Pull requests are not accepted** and will be closed without review. This repository is a download archive of the original map sources, maintained by KDany only.
- **Want to change a map?** Download or fork it and maintain your own version — the [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](./LICENSE) license allows this, as long as you give credit, stay non-commercial and share your version under the same license.
- **Found a bug or have feedback?** Report it in the dedicated issue tracker: [KDany/steam-workshop](https://github.com/KDany/steam-workshop/issues/new/choose). Fixes to the original maps are made by me.
- **Made a community variant?** Open a [Community variant listing](https://github.com/KDany/cs2-maps/issues/new?template=variant_listing.yml) issue and I will list it in the README.
- **Need permission beyond the license** (e.g. commercial use)? Open a [Permission request](https://github.com/KDany/cs2-maps/issues/new?template=permission_request.yml).
