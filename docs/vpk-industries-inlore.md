## Welcome to the world. Our world.

Every world you have ever walked through was built by someone. Most likely, by us.

Since the GoldSrc era, when we were still known as **BSP Industries** (*Binary Space Partitioning Industries*), we have divided empty space into places worth fighting over. Today, as **VPK Industries**, we do not just build worlds. We perfect them, package them and keep them running, round after round.

You never think about the weather on the map you play. **That is exactly how we like it.**

---

## Our history

**The GoldSrc era.** BSP Industries is founded with a simple mission: *partition space*. The first worlds are built one brush at a time.

**The Source 2 era.** The company becomes VPK Industries. Brushes become meshes, worlds become packages, and our ambitions become slightly larger than the worlds themselves.

**Project Aftershock.** Our first weather machine, the *Rainmaker Mk I*, completes its final test during the development of the Aftershock update. The test is described in *Incident Report #001* as "an unscheduled energetic event". The Rainmaker Mk I never saw daylight. We prefer to say it was *ahead of its time*.

**Today.** The *Rainmaker Mk II* has grown into **KD-WX-003 S.T.R.A.T.O.S.**, the heart of our Weather Program, and our particle laboratories produce the most advanced synthetic phenomena ever packaged into a world.

All VPK machines are designed by our Chief Designer, **KDany**, and operated exclusively by the Nonvis Division.
